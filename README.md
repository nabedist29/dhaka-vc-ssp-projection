# dhaka-vc-ssp-projection
A simple project looking at how two future climate scenarios, SSP245 and SSP585, might change dengue mosquito conditions in Dhaka District, compared to the historical period (1980–2015). Uses NASA climate model data and a simple Vectorial Capacity formula from mosquito-disease research.

# Data
Climate data: NASA GDDP-CMIP6, accessed through Google Earth Engine
Study area: Dhaka District, Bangladesh (~10 km grid)
Time periods: Historical (1980–2015), SSP245 and SSP585 (2020–2100)

# Limitations

Rainfall and temperature come from two different climate models, not a matching pair, so results could look different with another model combination.
The ~10 km grid is too coarse to say anything about specific neighborhoods within Dhaka.
Monthly rainfall is used as a rough stand-in for mosquito numbers and doesn't account for water storage habits, drainage, or larval development time.
